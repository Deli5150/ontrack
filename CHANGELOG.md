# Changelog

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-24

- Calendar on Mac, Windows and Linux: swipe sideways with two fingers on the trackpad, or press ← and →, to change month, as swiping does on phones (#3)

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-16

- Import from photos finds every subscription in a screenshot of a list (another subscription app, the App Store's Subscriptions page, a budgeting app), not just one (#1). Text is now read with each line's position on the screen, so a name and its price on the same row stay together
- Import a file reads spreadsheets that list subscriptions, bills or debts one per row, including Excel (.xlsx) files, instead of merging them into one subscription (#2). It picks the payment column over balances and interest rates, reads frequency, due date (or day of the month), currency and category columns, and puts loans, cards, rent and utilities in the right categories. OnTrack's own spreadsheet export imports back
- Receipts use the plan's price, not the tax, total or a date
- Calendar: swipe anywhere on the screen to change month, not just over the dates (#3)

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-14

First release of OnTrack for macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone and Android.

- Track every subscription: price, currency, payment date, cycle (every N days/weeks/months/years), one-time payments, duration, free trials, lists, categories, payment methods, URL and notes
- Spending ring: one slice per active subscription, sized by what it costs, with your monthly total
- 8 colour themes, built-in wallpapers or your own photo
- Desktop layout with a sidebar and side-by-side panes
- Calendar of payments with each month's total and what's still to come
- Add from 120+ popular services, type or paste ("Netflix 15.99 monthly"), import screenshots (on-device text recognition) or bank statements (CSV, and PDF on desktop)
- Official brand logos from SVGL, any website's icon, an icon, an emoji or your own picture
- Reminders before payments, billing and price history, 30+ currencies with daily exchange rates
- Sync with Google: the same subscriptions on every device, kept in a private folder in your Google Drive
- Backups (JSON), spreadsheet export (CSV), and in-app updates with an Update available sheet; on iPhone, update through AltStore, SideStore, Feather or FlareStore, or download the .ipa
- Report bugs and request features from Settings
