Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Your data stays yours

OnTrack stores your subscriptions, lists, categories, payment methods and settings on your own device. There are no OnTrack servers and no accounts, and nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Sync with Google (optional)

If you turn on Sync with Google, OnTrack asks for access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) and your email address (to show which account is syncing). OnTrack keeps one file there with your OnTrack data so your devices stay the same. It cannot see or touch any other files in your Drive. Turn Sync off at any time to sign out; you can delete the file by removing OnTrack under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

OnTrack’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connections

To work, OnTrack fetches brand logos (from SVGL via jsDelivr, and Google’s website-icon service), currency exchange rates (open.er-api.com) and update information (GitHub). These requests contain no personal data.

Photos and files

Screenshots and statements you import are read on your device and never uploaded.
